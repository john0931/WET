Mobile Kitchen Studio

This is a privacy-safe visual model for phone use. It contains no home address, names, email addresses or project budget.

  • Floor: mixed-format terracotta, represented as a modular field with a visible 45° chamfer at the hardwood transition.
  • Countertops: conceptual textures calibrated to the physical Prado and Musq samples. Full slab selection still governs.
  • Cabinet palette: cherry uppers, warm cream lowers, light hood and brass hardware.

For installation, the tile pattern should be dry-laid strategically from visible boundaries, especially the diagonal transition, to avoid awkward sliver cuts.
