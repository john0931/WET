About this model

This public/mobile version uses the current v.4 layout direction and removes personal information, address and budget data.

  • Natural Cream lower/tall cabinets with shaker-style fronts.
  • Cherry Floris-style uppers with glass and mullion detailing.
  • Custom light hood between the 24-inch glass uppers.
  • Apron sink, dishwasher, pantry, existing-style stainless appliances and dining-side peninsula shelves.
  • Mixed-format Valdorcia terracotta with the existing diagonal tile-to-hardwood transition represented.

Important: geometry is for design review, not fabrication. Field measurements, cabinet shop drawings, sink template and tile dry-lay govern construction.
